Control chart 3 — Consent SMS coverage (dual-number rule)

% of required unique numbers that received the SMS.

Coverage % Target 100%
Why it matters: Your rule: 1 SMS when applicant & co-applicant numbers match, 2 when they differ. If the system silently sends one SMS to two distinct numbers, consent from the co-applicant is never solicited — a compliance and dispute risk. This chart tracks "SMS sent ÷ SMS required", so de-duplication bugs surface immediately.

Why it matters: "Unmatched" payments (money received but not tied to a case) are the reconciliation blind spot NBFCs hit most. Each one risks a wrong void or a customer paying but not getting closed. Trend it weekly — a rising unmatched band means the matching rule or the LMS→Indus feed needs attention.
