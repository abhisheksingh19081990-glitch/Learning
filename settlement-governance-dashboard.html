Void tracking — including flag-sync false voids
Void reasonCasesType
No payment within 45 days42Genuine
Partial only, never reached full by +45d31Genuine
Full amount received in Indus, but voided because match flag did not sync to LMS7FALSE VOID
Why it matters: A false void is a case where the customer actually paid the full agreed amount into Indus, but the matched-flag didn’t sync to LMS in time, so the case auto-voided at +45 days and the paid amount got wrongly adjusted against overdue. These must be zero. Tracking them separately triggers reversal + re-settlement and quantifies the business cost of the flag-sync delay.
